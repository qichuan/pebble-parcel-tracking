Wondering if anything is arriving today? This app answers that question in an instant.

Open any shipment to check its current status, location, estimated delivery time, and complete past tracking history. Want an instant update? Just long-press to force a refresh.

Setup Note: This app requires your own free Ship24 API key (grab one for free at https://www.ship24.com/tracking-api). Enter your key in the app settings on your phone to get started 

Compatible with all Pebble smartwatches
